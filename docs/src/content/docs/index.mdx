## The Context Window

This field has a context window of its own: what you knew a month ago isn't wrong, it has simply scrolled out of attention, and the only way to stay in the window is to keep showing up.

This is the org's open forum for how we build with AI in a landscape that reinvents itself every few months. Staying current here isn't a nice-to-have. It's a discipline, and it's one we practice together.

## North Star: TTV (Tokens to Value)

Not minimizing token spend. Maximizing what every token buys us.
