## Getting Started

### Where should I start a discussion? Is Slack okay?

- Anywhere, including a conversation between you and your agent
- Slack is fine for early, informal ideas
- Once an idea feels adoptable, move it to a proposal
- Prefer GitHub Discussions for long-running threads, so they do not get lost

### Do I need to fill every stack slot?

- No
- Each slot describes a capability that has proven useful, not a mandatory checklist
- Fill the slots your immediate problem needs, using each slot's selection criteria

### I spend more time correcting output than writing it. Where do I start?

- Treat each repeated correction as an input bug, not a model failure
- Read in this order:
  1. [Delegation Fit](/SHRINE/patterns/delegation-fit/): before you prompt; some tasks cost more to review than to write
  2. [Reviewable Output](/SHRINE/patterns/reviewable-output/): what to ask for
  3. [Correction Diagnosis](/SHRINE/patterns/correction-diagnosis/): when it still misses; walk the cause chain, upstream first
- Measure before deciding: [Individual Baseline](/SHRINE/stack/evaluation/#individual-baseline) (draft guidance)

### Is LLM coding worth it?

- Sometimes; measure it on your own work rather than trusting anyone's headline number
- History: METR measured a 19% slowdown in 2025, then called its late-2025 rerun data unreliable ([details](/SHRINE/stack/evaluation/#measure-dont-ask))
- Survey (May 2026): 349 technical workers reported a median 1.4 to 2x change in the value of their work from AI; self-reported, not measured ([METR](https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-11-ai-usage-survey/))
- Some work costs more to delegate than to write: see the "Against delegating" list in [Delegation Fit](/SHRINE/patterns/delegation-fit/#fit-signals)
- Find out for your own tasks: [Individual Baseline](/SHRINE/stack/evaluation/#individual-baseline) (draft guidance)

### How does my team adopt SHRINE?

- Measure first: run the [Team Baseline](/SHRINE/stack/evaluation/#team-baseline) on two or three recurring workflows (draft guidance)
- Apply the top input fix the baseline surfaces; fill a [Stack](/SHRINE/stack/overview/) slot only if that fix lives in one
- Feed what you learn back through [Governance](/SHRINE/reference/governance/): a Discussion, then a proposal

### What's the relationship between SHRINE and my team's practices?

- SHRINE is a floor, not a ceiling
- The patterns here are a shared baseline
- Teams are free to extend, adapt, or add to them

## Proposals & Adoption

### What's the difference between a discussion and a proposal?

- A discussion explores an idea
- A proposal asks the community to adopt it
- Discussions can lead to proposals, but do not have to
- Not everything needs to become doctrine

### How does something become an official pattern or principle?

- One of three routes; the first two start with a proposal in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/stablekernel/SHRINE/discussions/categories/proposals):
  - [Lifecycle](/SHRINE/reference/governance/#proposal-lifecycle): two keeper endorsements, then a thirty-day objection window
  - [Expedited](/SHRINE/reference/governance/#expedited-ratification): the same, without the objection window
  - [Keeper decision](/SHRINE/reference/governance/#keeper-decision): a majority of keepers ratify or amend directly, and can waive the Discussion

### What's the difference between a Principle, a Pattern, and Stack?

- **Principle**: a commitment that decides tradeoffs; tool-agnostic; no "when not to use"
- **Pattern**: a technique with context, and a legitimate "when to use / when not to use"
- **Stack**: an abstract capability slot: what the capability must do, how to select for it, and anti-patterns; not a product list

Quick tests:

- Describes a capability the work needs, whatever fills it? Stack.
- Has a legitimate "when not to use"? Pattern.
- Violating it needs a recorded exception? Principle.

Patterns may name products as brief examples. Stack pages name no products, but may name open standards such as MCP and OpenTelemetry. Current product picks live in the [Current Stack Roster](/SHRINE/reference/current-stack/).

### Can I propose changes to existing patterns?

- Yes
- Open a Discussion referencing the page, then a PR with the change and its rationale ([Amendments](/SHRINE/reference/governance/#amendments))
- Patterns are living documents, not stone tablets

### How do I propose a change to the stack?

- Propose a change to a slot's definition or selection criteria
- Back it with demonstrated use: what you tried, what the capability needed, what you learned
- Adoption rests on demonstrated value, not theoretical promise
- To add, change, or drop a product row in the [Current Stack Roster](/SHRINE/reference/current-stack/), open a Discussion with the same evidence and a source link for the row

## Governance

### What if I disagree with a decision?

- Start a new discussion challenging it, referencing the original
- Decisions reflect current best judgment; better information changes them

### How do we deprecate a pattern or principle?

- Anyone can raise a pattern or principle for deprecation through a Discussion
- The community weighs in, and the governance lifecycle applies
- A stale `last-reviewed` date is one signal to raise it

### How do we handle vendor lock-in concerns?

- Case by case
- Weigh each tool's value against its maintenance overhead, coupling, and exit costs

### How do we balance innovation with stability?

- Fail fast on new concepts
- Tread carefully around well-refined, proven ones

### How do I become a keeper of the SHRINE?

- Participate: make your presence felt and your ideas heard
- The keeper pool stays proportionate to the community
- Enough keepers that no idea gets ignored; few enough that guardianship keeps its meaning
- Act like a keeper before anyone asks you to
