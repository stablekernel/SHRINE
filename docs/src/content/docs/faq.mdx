## Participation

### How do I become a keeper of the SHRINE?

Simply participate. Make your presence felt, your passion seen. The pool of keepers remains proportionate to our community: enough that no one bringing an idea to the table gets ignored, but not so many that it loses its purpose of guardianship. Be the embodiment. Assume the role without being asked.

### Where should I start a discussion? Is Slack okay?

Anywhere! Even if that's between you and your agent. But once you feel like you have something adoptable, that's when it needs to move to a proposal. We encourage long-standing discussions to take place in our GitHub forum so things do not get lost.

## Adoption & Governance

### How does something become an official pattern or principle?

Open a proposal in the GitHub forum. If there's community support and no blocking concerns after discussion, a keeper will merge it. Controversial items get more time; obvious wins move fast.

### Can I propose changes to existing patterns?

Yes. Open a PR with your changes and explain the rationale. Patterns are living documents, not stone tablets.

### What if I disagree with a decision?

Reopen the discussion with new evidence or a different angle. Decisions aren't permanent; they're current best judgment. Better information changes them.

## Tooling & Practice

### Do I need to use every tool in the stack?

No. The stack documents what we've found effective, not what's mandatory. Start with what solves your immediate problem.

### How do I suggest a new tool for the stack?

Use it first. If it proves valuable, write it up and propose it. We adopt based on demonstrated value, not theoretical promise.

### What's the relationship between SHRINE and my team's practices?

SHRINE is a floor, not a ceiling. Teams can extend, adapt, or add to these practices. The patterns here are shared baseline, not imposed constraints.

## Process

### What's the difference between a discussion and a proposal?

Discussions can lead to a formal proposal, but can also just be a discussion. Not everything needs to become doctrine.

### How do we handle vendor lock-in concerns?

Case by case. Maintenance overhead and coupling is a constant concern in our industry. Evaluate each tool on its own merits and exit costs.

### How do we deprecate a pattern or principle?

It is raised for deprecation by the community. We may later propose a cadence for reviewing current principles, patterns, and tooling.

### How do we balance innovation with stability?

Fail fast on new concepts. Tread lightly on well-refined and proven concepts.
