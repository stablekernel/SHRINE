# The Shrine

LLM governance and tooling strategy for the org.

---

This field has a context window of its own: what you knew a month ago isn't wrong, it has simply scrolled out of attention, and the only way to stay in the window is to keep showing up.

This is the org's open forum for how we build with AI in a landscape that reinvents itself every few months. Staying current here isn't a nice-to-have. It's a discipline, and it's one we practice together.

## North Star: TTV (Tokens to Value)

Not minimizing token spend. Maximizing what every token buys us.

- Every tool choice, workflow, and practice we discuss gets measured against that
- The goal is maximum value per token, not minimum token usage

## How This Works

- **The keepers of the shrine** share initiatives and direction here
- **The community builds and maintains** what it's meant to support
- **Disagreement is welcome.** Nobody sees the whole board alone

## Discussion Threads

2. **Knowing When to Re-evaluate:** What signals tell us it's time to revisit choices?
